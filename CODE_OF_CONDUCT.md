# Code of Conduct

RepoTend welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Please be respectful, patient, and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Disagreement about technical approaches is welcome; attacks on people are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask participants to change behavior, temporarily limit participation, or block accounts for serious or repeated violations.

To report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers privately through an available GitHub profile contact channel. If there is no suitable private contact route, you may open a **non-sensitive** issue requesting a private reporting method; do not publish personal details in an issue.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid exposing the reporter's identity without permission. This policy applies to RepoTend's project spaces.
